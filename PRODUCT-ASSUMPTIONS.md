Idea Title:
CampusLoop – Campus Life in One Place


1. Idea Summary:
CampusLoop is a platform where students can find campus events, workshops, hackathons, and club activities in one place. Students can view details, register, bookmark events, and track upcoming activities.
2. Idea Source:
The idea comes from the difficulty of finding campus activities when information is shared across different channels.
3. Target Users:
College students, college clubs, and event organisers.
4. Problem Statement:
Students often miss campus events, workshops, and hackathons because information is scattered across WhatsApp groups, social media, posters, and college announcements. There is no single platform where students can easily find and track all these opportunities.
5. Problem Validation:
We can ask students whether they have missed events or registration deadlines because they did not receive the information on time. Their responses can help us understand the problem.
6. Key Assumptions:
•	Students want all campus events in one place.
•	Students sometimes miss events because information is scattered.
•	Students would find event bookmarking and reminders useful.
•	Event organisers would be willing to share event details on a common platform.
7. What are the core features?
•	Event Discovery: Find campus events, workshops, hackathons, competitions, and club activities in one place. 
•	Search and Filters: Search events by category, date, and interests. 
•	Event Details: View event descriptions, venues, dates, and registration deadlines. 
•	Bookmarks: Save interesting events for later. 
•	Registration: Register for events through provided registration links. 
•	Reminders: Get reminders about upcoming events and registration deadlines. 
•	Organiser Dashboard: Allow college clubs and organisers to publish and manage events.
8. What will be included in your MVP?
•	Student sign-up and login. 
•	A centralised list of upcoming campus events. 
•	Event details, including date, time, venue, and registration link. 
•	Basic search and category filters. 
•	Bookmarking events. 
•	Event creation and management for authorised organisers. 
•	A basic admin panel to review and approve event listings.
9. What will NOT be included in the MVP?
•	AI-powered personalised event recommendations. 
•	Real-time chat between students and organisers. 
•	A built-in payment system for paid events. 
•	Advanced event analytics and reporting. 
•	Attendance tracking using QR codes. 
•	Digital certificates for participation. 
•	Integration with every college's existing management system.
